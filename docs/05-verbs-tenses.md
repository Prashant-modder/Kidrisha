# Kidrisha'Chandra' — Verbs & Tenses

How will a verb get its distinctive form?

## Base System

Every verb will have a set of words added after it to create its person, tense, and mood/interjection.

## Persons

- **First Person** *(singular only)*: the single speaker performing the action.
- **Second Person**: the person or group the speaker is directly talking to.
- **Third Person Proximate**: the primary focal character or main entity outside the immediate conversation.
- **Clusivity Split** *(dual/trial & plural only)*:
  - **Inclusive**: a group of the speaker AND the listener. *"You and I are doing this together."*
  - **Exclusive**: a group of the speaker and their friends, BUT NOT the listener. *"My team is doing this, but you are left out."*
- **Logophoric Person**: used strictly inside reported speech or indirect quotes, to clarify that the person speaking the quote is talking about themselves. It removes any "he said that he" confusion.
